## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Minimalism** with a tactile, literary **Warm Editorial** sensibility. Designed for an independent bookstore, archive, and intellectual salon, the visual language balances the timelessness of physical bound paper with contemporary digital clarity.

### Personality & Values
- **Literary & Cultivated:** Speaks with quiet confidence, intellectual rigor, and reverence for words and craftsmanship.
- **Warm & Welcoming:** Avoids clinical starkness; evokes the sensory comfort of aged paperbacks, wood bookshelves, and linen book covers.
- **Uncluttered & Disciplined:** Generous margins, disciplined typography pairings, and hairline boundaries give space for literature, curations, and community programs to breathe.

### Design Principles
1. **Paper as Surface:** Interfaces behave like spreads in an art book or fine publication rather than purely digital slabs.
2. **Typographic Hierarchy over Decoration:** Structure is established primarily through scale, weight, and contrast of type, not superficial ornaments.
3. **Restrained Tactility:** Delicate borders, warm neutral layering, and rich espresso ink contrast replace artificial blurs and heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette derives its tonal depth from natural parchment, deckle-edge paper, linen canvas, and deep soot-based printing ink.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Ink (`#2B2520`):** A deep, warm charcoal espresso serving as the primary text color and foundational interactive state. Offers maximum optical contrast while remaining gentler on the eye than synthetic `#000000`.
- **Secondary Ink (`#594D43`):** Warm roasted brown used for subheadings, metadata, timestamps, and secondary icon actions.
- **Tertiary Tone (`#8A7D71`):** A soft umber used for subtle auxiliary annotations, chapter markings, and disabled states.
- **Neutral Canvas Core (`#EBE2CC`):** The primary brand neutral reflecting raw rag paper, anchoring cards, sidebars, and structural dividers.

### Contextual Named Hues
- **`paper-canvas` (`#FBF9F5`):** The default screen background, presenting an eye-resting, warm vellum appearance.
- **`paper-subtle` (`#EFE9DB`):** Soft container surface for cards, reading blocks, and elevated modules.
- **`paper-border` (`#DED6C1`):** Hairline dividing tone for structural separation without visual clutter.
- **`espresso-deep` (`#261F1A`) & `ivory-bright` (`#FAF7F2`):** High-contrast pairing strictly reserved for primary call-to-action buttons, badges, and announcement banners.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary weight of **Merriweather** (editorial serif) with the legibility and human warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** (clean modern sans-serif).

### Editorial Roles
- **Headlines & Long-Form Reading:** Handled by Merriweather. Headings adopt a deliberate, bookish cadence with generous line heights to emulate bespoke editorial layouts. `body-lg` in Merriweather is reserved for book descriptions, reviews, excerpts, and curatorial statements.
- **Interface & Operational Typography:** Handled by Plus Jakarta Sans. Ensures clean scanning across catalog listings, shopping filters, member dashboard details, metadata badges, and form controls.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in uppercase or small track-expanded Plus Jakarta Sans (`label-sm`), providing crisp taxonomy markers (e.g., "EDISI PERTAMA", "ARCHIVE", "SALON DISCUSSION").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the proportions of classic book design, prioritizing wide outer margins and generous gutters that prevent visual congestion.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive grid with a max container width of `1280px`. Outer margins sit at `3rem` (`48px`) with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Editorial reading containers are capped at 6 to 8 columns (`680px` to `760px`) to preserve optimal line lengths (55–75 characters).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacks sidebars and multi-column catalog items into single or double-column cards.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Rooted in an 8px spacing rhythm. Sections maintain generous separation (`space-xl` or `4rem+`) to preserve an unhurried, contemplative pace throughout the browsing experience.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-gloss surfaces, synthetic neon glows, and dramatic drop shadows. Visual depth is rendered through **tonal paper layering** and **fine architectural borders**.

### Depth Modalities
1. **Tonal Planar Stacking:**
   - **Base Canvas:** `#FBF9F5` (warm cream vellum).
   - **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Drawers):** `#EFE9DB` with a 1px border of `#DED6C1`.
   - **Level 2 (Active States, Floating Overlays, Modals):** `#FAF7F2` with a perimeter stroke of `#CFC6B1` and an ambient, low-contrast shadow.
2. **Ambient Paper Shadow:**
   When floating menus, search dropdowns, or reading drawers require separation from the underlying plane, use a diffused, warm-tinted shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -8px rgba(43, 37, 32, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 37, 32, 0.04);`
3. **Hairline Dividers:**
   Content sections are delineated using delicate 1px borders (`#DED6C1` or `#CFC6B1`). Never use heavy 2px or colored dividers.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometry, reflecting the slightly eased corners of bound book spines, heavy paper stock, and archival index cards.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Chips, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Maintains structural discipline and architectural dignity without appearing sharp or brutalist.
- **Medium Containers (Book Cards, Event Panels, Content Drawers):** `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Genre chips, reading status tags ("Sedang Dibaca"), and metadata counters may utilize fully rounded pill caps (`9999px`) only when paired with subtle backgrounds to avoid visual dominance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Surface in `espresso-deep` (`#261F1A`) with typography in `ivory-bright` (`#FAF7F2`). Border radius `4px`. Padding `12px 24px`. Hover state softly transitions background to `olive-umber` (`#3D352E`).
- **Secondary Button:** Surface in `transparent` with a 1px border in `#CFC6B1` and text in `#2B2520`. Hover fills the surface with `#EFE9DB`.
- **Text Button / Editorial Link:** Plain text in `#2B2520` accompanied by a continuous 1px underline positioned with a 4px offset. Hover shifts underline color to `#8A7D71`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Background `#EFE9DB`, border 1px solid `#DED6C1`, text `#594D43`, label size `12px` font weight `600`.
- **Selected State:** Background `#2B2520`, border 1px solid `#2B2520`, text `#FAF7F2`.
- Used for literary genres (e.g., *Filsafat*, *Sastra Klasik*, *Arsip Budaya*), salon event dates, and collection filters.

### Cards (Book & Exhibition Displays)
- Built on `#EFE9DB` with a 1px border of `#DED6C1`.
- **Aspect Ratios:** Book cover previews follow traditional book proportions (`2:3` or `3:4`).
- **Inner Padding:** `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Content Hierarchy:** Overline genre category in `label-sm` (`#8A7D71`), title in `headline-sm` Merriweather (`#2B2520`), author in `body-sm` (`#594D43`), followed by subtle availability badge.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Background:** `#FAF7F2` against `#FBF9F5` canvas for crisp legibility.
- **Border:** 1px solid `#DED6C1`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#2B2520` with no synthetic outer glow ring; an understated 1px offset ring in `#EBE2CC` preserves accessibility.
- **Placeholder Text:** `#8A7D71` in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom controls using `#FAF7F2` container with a 1px solid `#CFC6B1` outline.
- Selected state fills the control with `#261F1A`, featuring an `#FAF7F2` check or center pip. Radii are `2px` for checkboxes and `50%` for radios.

### Specialized Components
- **Reading Room Excerpt Block:** Indented container surfaced in `#EBE2CC` with a 2px left border in `#594D43`. Set in `body-lg` Merriweather italic.
- **Catalog Shelf Index:** Minimalist table-like row layout with hairline dividers, aligning ISBN, Title, Author, Shelf Location, and Lending Status with spacious horizontal tracking.
